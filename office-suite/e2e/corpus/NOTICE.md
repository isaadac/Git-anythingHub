# Test corpus

Real-world Word, Excel and PowerPoint files used by `e2e/suites/corpus.js`. They come from the
Apache POI project's test data (https://github.com/apache/poi/tree/trunk/test-data), files that
were made by Microsoft Office and other programs and collected from bug reports.
